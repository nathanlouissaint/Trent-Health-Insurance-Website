# Visual direction

Reference reviewed: HealthSherpa public homepage, October 26, 2026.

## Characteristics translated for Trent's portfolio

- **Color relationship:** a deep indigo anchor paired with pale lavender, airy blue, soft mint, warm off-white, and occasional peach accent. The portfolio uses `brand`, `brandDark`, `ink`, `lavender`, `sky`, `mint`, `cream`, `lilac`, and `coral` Tailwind tokens.
- **Typography:** a compact, confident sans-serif display face for headings with an accessible, highly readable sans-serif body. Headings use tight tracking and generous scale; body copy stays restrained and conversational.
- **Rhythm:** large, breathable vertical sections (5rem mobile / 7rem desktop), wide content rails, and a bold two-column hero with a nearly square visual panel.
- **Shapes:** rounded pills for calls to action, large 30–40px card radii, modest borders, and soft layered color shapes rather than hard-edged panels.
- **Cards:** light, friendly color fields with simple original line icons, restrained shadows, and small lift interactions.
- **Navigation:** a slim sticky navigation bar with a name-led mark, simple text links, and one prominent booking CTA.
- **Imagery:** spacious human-centered image placements. Trent's real portrait is deliberately represented by clearly labeled placeholders until an approved image is supplied; no third-party illustration or fabricated portrait is used.

This implementation is original and intentionally avoids HealthSherpa branding, logos, illustrations, copy, and page composition.
